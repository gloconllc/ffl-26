Settings


      

        
Strategy

        
This setting is shared across the whole app — Draft, Lineup, Trades, and Free Agents will all read from it once built, not just this tab.

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        
"Recommended" always ranks an open roster need above pure score. "The Brain" ranks purely by score (VORP/tier) and will happily recommend the highest-value player at a position you don't need — that's not a bug, it's the most-points-no-matter-what mode. "Custom" blends the two by the slider above (0 = pure Brain, 100 = full Recommended).

      


      

        
Scoring tier weights

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        
All four tiers are real and combined into each player's Score (0-100) by these weights — turning Risk all the way up and everything else down, for example, ranks purely by health/durability. Contextual is currently Week 1 2026 schedule/odds only (not yet full-season weather/coaching/matchup intelligence — see docs/CONTEXT.md). Sliders persist to storage automatically.
